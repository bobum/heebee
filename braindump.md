# Story Braindump

## Setting/Locations

- Rural town in the hills/mountains (similar to Helen, villages/towns up in Oklahoma(?))
- Takes place mostly in the outskirts of the town (mechanic's shop, old village house, cafe, shop under renovation)

## General Spitballing Ideas

- A woman returning to her old hometown after a particularly bad accident on a motorcycle and having to stay with her parents. Re-meeting up with some old friends and learning recent town drama, but her memory of the town is tainted with something that feels wrong

- Slowly unraveling some interesting things about the town that nobody seems to really bat an eye at (missing persons, strange things happening in houses and in the town square, strange "habits" that nobody remembers forming or breaking) and investigating what exactly is going on

- Town is under some sort of "siege" or "contract" by an otherworldly entity (possibly a physical/emotional manifestation of superstitions mixed with the town's negative emotion?) and each of her friends is "contracted" to it (read: emotionally attached to it) in some way. The town as a whole feeds into it by their superstitions while still not "believing" in its existence

- Having to help each friend come to terms with the event tying them to this overbearing creature of guilt/grief/rage/etc and regulate themselves to try and figure out how to rid themselves of it?

- **If successful:** Manage to leave the town again with some of the friends
- **If a partial fail (unable to save some of them):** Choose to stay with them or leave them behind?
- **A total fail:** Getting stuck/trapped in the town, maybe an epilogue showing the protagonist going about daily movements in the same way everyone else did when she first arrived

## Characters

### Main Characters

- **Harper Mosley** - The woman from the outside
- **Wynona Warner** - The local mechanic
- **Finnegan Caulfield** - Similar to Harper's predicament, recently moved back to the town after a period of disappearance and is having a rough time fitting back in
- **Murphy Hewitt** - Harper's old high school friend whose brother recently went missing
- **Lorelaine DeFrees** - Local unenthused barista at the nearby cafe
- Maybe others?

### Antagonist

- **The Inhabitant** - The creature of festering negativity that exists as both a concept and physical threat
