# Story Braindump

## Premise

A woman returns to her childhood hometown after a severe motorcycle accident leaves her unable to afford rent or hospital bills. She moves back in with her parents who live in a small mountain town.

Upon returning, she finds the town unsettling—her childhood memories are either missing entirely or colored by negative emotions she can't quite explain.

## Setting

- Small mountain town
- Isolated, with something preventing residents from wanting to leave
- Has a strange effect on people's memories

## Main Character

- Woman recovering from a serious motorcycle accident
- Financially struggling (can't afford apartment rent or medical bills)
- Has fragmented or negative memories of her hometown
- Eventually becomes one of the few people who can perceive the true nature of what's happening

## Supporting Characters

| Character | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **The Returnee** | A man who has also recently come back to town, similar situation to the protagonist |
| **The Mechanic** | Local mechanic, was one of her high school best friends |
| **The Barista** | Works at a local café, moonlights as a paranormal investigator |
| **The Brother** | Man on the outskirts of town whose brother has recently gone missing; strange things happening in and around his house |

## Inciting Incident

The story kicks off when the protagonist meets the man on the outskirts of town. His brother has gone missing, and inexplicable changes have been occurring in and around his home. This mystery draws the protagonist and her companions together.

## Central Mystery

- Something is living in or over the town
- It feeds on the residents' negative emotions: grief, guilt, and similar feelings
- It affects people's memories and suppresses their desire to leave
- Most people can't see it for what it truly is

## Core Gameplay Loop

- Investigate the strange occurrences with your group of companions
- Uncover the personal mysteries affecting each companion
- The more you discover about each companion's story, the clearer the bigger picture becomes
- Work to rid the town of the creature

## Themes

- Memory and its unreliability
- Grief and guilt as forces that trap us
- Returning home and confronting the past
- Seeing what others cannot (or will not) see
- Community and connection as tools against darkness
