# Story Braindump

## Premise

A woman returns to her childhood hometown after a severe motorcycle accident leaves her unable to afford rent or hospital bills. She moves back in with her parents who live in a small mountain town.

Upon returning, she finds the town unsettling—her childhood memories are either missing entirely or colored by negative emotions she can't quite explain.

## Setting/Locations

- Rural town in the hills/mountains (similar to Helen, GA or villages/towns up in Oklahoma)
- Isolated, with something preventing residents from wanting to leave
- Has a strange effect on people's memories
- Key locations mostly on the outskirts of town:
  - Mechanic's shop
  - Old village house
  - Café
  - Shop under renovation

## Characters

### Protagonist

- **Harper Mosley** - The woman from the outside
  - Recovering from a serious motorcycle accident
  - Financially struggling (can't afford apartment rent or medical bills)
  - Has fragmented or negative memories of her hometown
  - Eventually becomes one of the few people who can perceive the true nature of what's happening

### Main Characters

| Character | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Wynona Warner** | The local mechanic; one of Harper's old high school best friends |
| **Finnegan Caulfield** | Similar to Harper's predicament—recently moved back to town after a period of disappearance, having a rough time fitting back in |
| **Murphy Hewitt** | Harper's old high school friend whose brother recently went missing |
| **Lorelaine DeFrees** | Local unenthused barista at the nearby café; paranormal investigator on the side |

### Antagonist

- **The Inhabitant** - The creature of festering negativity that exists as both a concept and physical threat
  - Lives in or over the town
  - Feeds on residents' negative emotions: grief, guilt, rage
  - Affects people's memories and suppresses their desire to leave
  - The town feeds into it through superstitions while not "believing" in its existence
  - Each companion is "contracted" to it (emotionally attached) in some way

## Inciting Incident

The story kicks off when Harper meets Murphy on the outskirts of town. His brother has recently gone missing, and inexplicable changes have been occurring in and around his home. This mystery draws Harper and her companions together.

## Story Threads

- Slowly unraveling interesting things about the town that nobody seems to bat an eye at:
  - Missing persons
  - Strange things happening in houses and in the town square
  - Strange "habits" that nobody remembers forming or breaking
- Having to help each friend come to terms with the event tying them to The Inhabitant
- Helping companions regulate themselves to figure out how to rid the town of this creature

## Possible Endings

- **Success:** Manage to leave the town again with some of the friends
- **Partial Failure:** Unable to save some companions—choose to stay with them or leave them behind?
- **Total Failure:** Getting stuck/trapped in the town; epilogue showing Harper going about daily movements in the same way everyone else did when she first arrived

## Core Gameplay Loop

- Investigate the strange occurrences with your group of companions
- Uncover the personal mysteries affecting each companion
- The more you discover about each companion's story, the clearer the bigger picture becomes
- Work to rid the town of The Inhabitant

## Themes

- Memory and its unreliability
- Grief and guilt as forces that trap us
- Returning home and confronting the past
- Seeing what others cannot (or will not) see
- Community and connection as tools against darkness
- Superstition vs. belief
